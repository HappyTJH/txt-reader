# Code of Conduct

TXT Reader is a small open source project, and we want it to stay welcoming and
practical.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about problems.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.
- Respect privacy when sharing sample TXT files or reproduction data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Posting private information without permission
- Repeatedly derailing issues or pull requests
- Sharing malicious files or exploit details outside a responsible disclosure
  process

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may limit participation when
behavior makes the project unsafe or unproductive.

Security issues should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
